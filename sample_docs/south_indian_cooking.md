# South Indian breakfast basics

This file is unrelated to RAG on purpose. Jev should score these chunks low for RAG questions, which shows how irrelevant candidates get dropped before the LLM sees them.

## Idli batter

Soak 3 cups of idli rice and 1 cup of urad dal separately for 6 hours. Grind the dal with cold water until fluffy, then grind the rice to a slightly coarse paste. Mix both with salt and let the batter ferment overnight in a warm place until it doubles in volume.

## Dosa

Use the same batter, thinned with a little water. Heat a cast iron tawa, wipe it with a halved onion dipped in oil, pour a ladle of batter and spread it in circles. Drizzle ghee around the edges and cook until crisp and golden.

## Coconut chutney

Grind fresh grated coconut with roasted chana dal, green chillies, a small piece of ginger and salt. Temper with mustard seeds, curry leaves and a dried red chilli in coconut oil.

## Sambar

Cook toor dal until soft. Boil drumstick, shallots and tomato in tamarind water with sambar powder, add the dal, simmer, and finish with a tempering of mustard seeds, fenugreek and asafoetida.
